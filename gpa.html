Important: This calculator uses a common 5-point scale: A = 5, B = 4, C = 3, D = 2, E = 1, F = 0. Universities may use different grading systems. Check your school's handbook for its official rules.

How GPA is calculated

For each course, multiply its credit units by the grade point earned. Add the quality points for all courses, then divide by the total credit units.

Formula: GPA = Total quality points ÷ Total credit units
