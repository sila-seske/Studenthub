Contact StudentHub

Questions, bug reports, feature ideas and privacy requests all go to the same inbox. Pick the topic below to start a pre-filled email.

General question

Anything about how StudentHub works or what it does.

Bug or problem

Something is not working as expected on your device.

Privacy request

Questions about your data, cookies or your rights.

Lecturer question

Help with the teaching schedule or course materials.

Email us directly

Write to getstudenthub@gmail.com. We aim to reply within five working days.

What to include

  • For bugs, tell us your device, your browser and what you were doing when the problem happened.
  • Please do not send your timetable, grades, expenses or other personal details unless they are needed to solve the problem.
  • Your app data lives on your device, so we cannot retrieve it from our side. For data problems, check your browser’s site storage first.

Privacy requests

We hold very little personal information, mostly email correspondence. For information held by Google, such as analytics and ad data, contact Google directly. Our privacy policy explains your rights under the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023.
