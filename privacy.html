Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 October 2026

StudentHub is a student-focused platform designed to help students organize their academic activities, plan study sessions, manage relevant personal records and discover learning resources.

This Privacy Policy explains what information may be handled when you visit or use StudentHub, how that information may be used, and the choices available to you.

By using StudentHub, you acknowledge this policy. Where applicable law requires consent for a particular type of data processing, tracking technology or advertising cookie, we will seek that consent rather than relying solely on continued use of the website.

1. Information We May Collect

The information handled by StudentHub depends on the features you use and how the website is configured. It may include the following categories:

  • Information you enter: for example, a name, study preference, class details, course information, tasks or expense records that you choose to enter into the application.
  • Technical information: information that a web server or service may receive during a visit, such as an IP address, browser type, device information, referring page and request time.
  • Usage information: information about how visitors interact with pages or features, where an analytics service is enabled.
  • Messages you send us: information you choose to include when contacting StudentHub for support or feedback.

StudentHub should only describe information as collected if the website or a service it uses actually receives that information. We do not intend this list to imply that every category is collected from every visitor.

2. Information Stored on Your Device

Some StudentHub features may use browser storage, including localStorage, to remember information you enter and settings you select.

Depending on the features you use, locally stored information may include:

  • Your display name and study preferences.
  • Your class schedule and related class details.
  • Study plans, tasks or other saved preferences.
  • Expense records or other information entered into a feature.

Information stored only in your browser is not automatically uploaded to a StudentHub server simply because it exists on your device. However, information may be sent elsewhere if you use a feature or third-party service that transmits it.

Browser storage may remain after you close the website. You can generally clear site data through your browser's settings. Doing so may remove saved information and application preferences.

Do not enter passwords, banking credentials or other highly sensitive information into features that are not designed to handle it securely.

3. How We May Use Information

Where applicable to the features you use, information may be processed for purposes such as:

  • Providing the features and resources you request.
  • Remembering your preferences and application settings.
  • Understanding website usage and improving usability.
  • Responding to questions, feedback and support requests.
  • Maintaining website security and diagnosing technical issues.
  • Displaying advertising if advertising services are enabled.
  • Meeting applicable legal obligations.

We will not claim to perform an activity that the website does not actually perform.

4. Google Analytics

StudentHub may use Google Analytics to understand how visitors use the website, such as which pages are visited and how people interact with its features.

When Google Analytics is enabled, Google may process technical and usage information through cookies or similar technologies, subject to the configuration of the service, applicable consent requirements and Google's own terms and privacy practices.

Analytics data may help us understand website performance and improve the experience for students.

For more information, read Google's Privacy Policy and Google's information about Google Analytics .

This section applies when Google Analytics is actually enabled on the website. Its presence in this policy does not establish that every visit is tracked.

5. Cookies and Similar Technologies

Cookies are small files that websites or third-party services may store in your browser. Similar technologies may also be used to remember settings, measure activity or support advertising.

Depending on the services enabled, cookies or similar technologies may be used to:

  • Support essential website functions.
  • Remember selected settings.
  • Measure website traffic and usage.
  • Support advertising and measure advertising performance.

You can review and manage cookies through your browser settings. Blocking certain cookies may affect some website features.

Where consent is legally required for non-essential cookies or similar technologies, those technologies should not be activated before the required consent is obtained.

6. Google AdSense and Advertising

StudentHub may display advertisements provided by Google AdSense or other advertising partners if these services are enabled.

Advertising providers may use cookies or similar technologies to display, deliver, measure or personalize advertisements, subject to applicable law, user choices and the provider's settings.

The information processed can depend on the advertising configuration, consent choices and whether personalized advertising is available. We do not promise that every advertisement is personalized or that every visitor will see the same advertisements.

You can learn more about how Google uses information on partner sites by visiting How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services .

You can also review Google's advertising technologies and Google's Ads Settings .

If StudentHub uses advertising that requires a consent message or privacy choices mechanism, we will implement the appropriate mechanism for the relevant visitors and advertising configuration.

7. When Information May Be Shared

StudentHub does not need to share every item entered into the application with an outside party. However, information may be processed by third parties when necessary for services that are actually enabled on the website.

Examples may include:

  • Analytics providers: to measure website usage where analytics is enabled.
  • Advertising providers: to deliver or measure advertisements where advertising is enabled.
  • Hosting and technical providers: to deliver the website, maintain its availability and protect it from abuse.
  • External resource providers: when you follow a link to another website.
  • Legal authorities: where disclosure is required by applicable law or necessary to address a legitimate legal obligation.

Third-party services operate under their own applicable privacy policies and terms. We cannot control every practice of an external website or service.

8. External Links and Learning Resources

StudentHub may link to educational websites, libraries, learning platforms and other external resources.

When you open an external website, that website's own privacy policy and terms apply to your use of it. Review those documents before submitting personal information to another service.

9. Data Retention and Deletion

Information stored in your browser may remain there until you delete it, clear the site's storage or remove the relevant application data.

Information received through emails, forms, analytics, hosting or other enabled services may be retained for the time reasonably necessary for the relevant purpose, subject to applicable legal requirements and the policies of those services.

If StudentHub receives personal information that you want corrected or deleted, you may contact us using the details provided below. We will review the request and explain any applicable limitations.

10. Security

We aim to use reasonable measures to protect the website and any personal information we handle.

However, no website, device, storage system or transmission over the internet can be guaranteed to be completely secure. Users should avoid submitting sensitive information unless the relevant feature is designed to handle it appropriately.

11. Children's Privacy

StudentHub is designed for students, who may include people under the age of 18.

We aim to limit the information requested to what is reasonably needed for the relevant features. We do not knowingly ask children to provide unnecessary sensitive personal information.

Where parental consent or another safeguard is required by applicable law, the relevant requirements must be followed. Advertising and analytics practices must also comply with the rules applicable to children and other protected users.

If you believe a child has provided personal information inappropriately, please contact us so that we can review the situation.

12. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on the laws that apply to you, you may have rights to request access to personal information, correct inaccurate information, request deletion, object to certain processing or withdraw consent where consent is the basis for processing.

To make a privacy request, contact StudentHub using the contact details below. We may need to ask for reasonable information to verify and process a request.

We will handle requests in accordance with applicable data protection laws, including relevant Nigerian data protection requirements where they apply.

13. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the website's features, service providers, advertising configuration or legal obligations change.

When we make changes, we will update the date at the top of this page. We encourage visitors to review this policy periodically.

14. Contact StudentHub

If you have questions, concerns or requests relating to this Privacy Policy or your personal information, please contact the StudentHub team.
