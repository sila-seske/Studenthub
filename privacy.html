Privacy policy

1. Who we are

StudentHub is a free planning app for students and lecturers. It is available at mystudenthubapp.com and can be installed on your phone or computer. It runs in your web browser and has no user accounts. Questions about this policy can be sent to getstudenthub@gmail.com.

2. Data stored on your device

The information you enter is saved in your browser’s local storage on your device. This includes your name and role, your classes and timetable, exam dates, expenses, GPA entries, study preferences, course materials you add as a lecturer, and your settings.

We do not receive this information. We cannot see it, recover it or back it up for you. Clearing your browser’s site data, or choosing Erase all data in Settings, deletes it permanently. Anyone who can use your device or browser profile can see it, so keep your device locked.

3. Google Analytics

This site uses Google Analytics 4 to understand how people use our pages. It collects information such as the pages you visit, how long you stay, your browser and device type, your approximate location based on your IP address, and the site that referred you.

Google Analytics does not receive the entries you make in the app, such as your timetable, grades or expenses. Google processes analytics data under its own privacy policy.

4. Google AdSense and advertising

We show advertisements through Google AdSense, which helps keep StudentHub free. Google and its partners use cookies and similar technologies to show ads, measure how they perform and, where permitted, show ads based on your visits to this and other sites. Google’s DART cookie is one of the cookies used to serve and measure ads.

You can manage personalised advertising in Google’s Ads Settings at adssettings.google.com. You can also opt out of many third-party ad networks at aboutads.info. If you opt out, you will still see ads, but they will not be based on your interests.

6. Your choices

  • Turn off personalised ads in Google’s Ads Settings.
  • Block or delete cookies in your browser. This does not delete the app data stored on your device, though some ad and analytics features may stop working.
  • Erase your app data at any time from Settings. A private browsing window also discards local data when you close it.
  • Stop using the app at any time. We hold no account that would need closing.

7. Your rights under Nigerian law

The Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 gives you rights over your personal data. These include the right to be informed, to access and correct it, to have it erased, to object to processing, and to withdraw consent. For information held by Google, contact Google directly. For anything else, email us and we will respond within the time the Act allows.

If you believe your rights have not been respected, you can lodge a complaint with the Nigeria Data Protection Commission.

8. Children

StudentHub is intended for students aged 13 and over. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. Users under 18 should use StudentHub with the approval of a parent or guardian. If you believe a child has given us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

9. Where data is processed

Google Analytics and Google AdSense process data on servers in several countries, including countries outside Nigeria, under safeguards described in Google’s policies. Your StudentHub app data never leaves your device.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the app or our advertising arrangements change. The date at the top of this page shows when it last changed.

11. Contact us

Email getstudenthub@gmail.com with the subject “Privacy request”. We aim to reply within five working days.
