About StudentHub

StudentHub is a free planner for students and lecturers. It brings your classes, exams, study time, spending and course materials into one simple app that runs in your browser.

A simple idea

Your study life is personal. StudentHub is built so your timetable, exam dates, expenses and grades stay on your device, and you do not need an account to start. It is designed with students in mind, including the everyday things that matter in Nigeria, such as naira budgeting and the common 5-point GPA scale.

What you can do

Today at a glance

Today’s classes, your next exam, your next study session and what you have spent this month.

Timetable and exams

A weekly timetable with clash warnings, an exam countdown and reminders before classes and exams.

Study planner

Turns your classes and exams into study sessions around the times of day you prefer.

Money tracker

Log expenses by category, set a monthly budget and see what is left.

GPA calculator

Work out your semester GPA from your grades and course units on the common 5-point scale.

Lecturer tools

Lecturers get a teaching schedule, reminders and a place to add links and notes for students.

How it works

  • Open StudentHub in any modern browser and start. There is no sign-up.
  • Install it to your home screen with the Install app button on the Today page, or from your browser’s menu.
  • After your first visit, the app loads offline from your device.

How StudentHub is funded

StudentHub is free to use. We cover the costs through advertising by Google AdSense. Our privacy policy explains exactly what Google collects, and you can limit personalised ads in your Google settings.

What StudentHub is not

StudentHub is not an official university system, a learning platform or a source of academic records. Confirm important dates and results with your institution.
